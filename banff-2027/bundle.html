Best timing

The value window is early June

June 4 to 9, 2027 currently gives the strongest mix of temperatures above your 60°F target, lower lodging than peak summer, and a safer cushion after the golf course's weather-dependent spring opening.

Best current balance

Pick June 4 to 9, 2027

May flights were cheaper, but Banff's average May high is below 60°F and the Stanley Thompson 18 opened on May 27 in 2026. Early June keeps most of the airfare advantage while materially reducing course-opening and cold-weather risk.

Important: an average high above 60°F does not guarantee every day will reach 60°F. Mountain weather can change quickly, and mornings can still be cold.

Getting around

Use a shuttle and transit unless the group wants maximum flexibility
